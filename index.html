Firmware for the M-VAVE FM-1

Gadlele

Three new instruments for the same small box. Each one replaces the FM-1's firmware, installs from the browser over USB in Chrome or Edge, and can be tried first in an emulator on its website.

A ghostly, ambient synth: drones, bells, rain and Omnichord chords, in a neon skin.
